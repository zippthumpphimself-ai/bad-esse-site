05 — FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Do you unlock phones?

We do software repair: bricked devices, bootloops, and activation or account locks on devices you own. Carrier unlocks are different — Canadian carriers have had to unlock your phone free on request since December 2017, so if that's all you need, we'll tell you to call your carrier instead of paying us.

How much is a screen?

Cost of the screen, plus $30 labour and a $5 shop fee. Text us the model and you'll have a number in minutes.

How do tattoos work with you?

Text us the idea, size and placement. We quote it, book it with a deposit, and the design gets approved before anything happens.

Can you recover my photos?

Often, yes, when the storage hardware is intact. Dead storage chips are the honest exception, and we'll say so before charging you anything.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins welcome — text or call first so the part or the chair is ready when you arrive. Shop 10am to 7pm, texts answered 11am to 7pm.
